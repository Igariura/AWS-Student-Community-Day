About The Event

AWS Student Community Day Kenya 2026 is a full-day immersive experience designed to empower students with cutting-edge cloud computing knowledge and skills. This event brings together over 250 students from leading universities across Kenya to explore Amazon Web Services, connect with industry professionals, and gain practical experience that will shape their future careers in technology.

💭

DREAM

Be inspired by keynote speakers who have transformed their cloud computing dreams into reality. Learn from AWS experts and industry leaders who will share their journeys and vision for the future of cloud technology.

🔨

BUILD

Get hands-on experience with AWS services through interactive workshops and live coding sessions. Deploy your first cloud application, explore serverless architecture, and master essential cloud development skills.

📈

SCALE

Network with fellow students, industry professionals, and AWS community builders. Discover career opportunities, join user groups, and become part of Kenya's growing cloud computing ecosystem.
